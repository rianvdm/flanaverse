# The Flanagan repertory: people, stories, and connective tissue

Research date: 2026-11-02. Scope: the narrative and human side of Mike Flanagan's recurring troupe. Exact credit overlaps should come from TMDB; this file covers who these people are, how they arrived, and the stories behind the overlaps. Claims carry a source link. Anything I could not confirm is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## 0. Corrections to the brief, and data-hygiene notes for the TMDB overlap engine

These affect how the site computes overlaps, so they come first.

- **Katie Parker and Catherine Parker are the same person.** TMDB person 551462 lists "Catherine Parker" as an alias and holds Absentia, Oculus, Hill House (Poppy), Doctor Sleep (Silent Sarey), Bly Manor (Perdita) and Usher (Annabel Lee) under one ID. Wikipedia's Bly Manor page uses both spellings. Do not count them as two actors. ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/551462-katie-parker), [Wikipedia: Bly Manor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Bly_Manor))
- **Chris Sumpter and William Chris Sumpter are the same person** (Spencer in The Midnight Club). TMDB credits him as William Chris Sumpter. ([TMDB search](https://www.themoviedb.org/search/person?query=Chris%20Sumpter))
- **Annarah Cymone is also credited as Annarah Shephard.** ([TMDB search](https://www.themoviedb.org/search/person?query=Annarah%20Cymone), [Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series)))
- **James Flanagan and Jamie Flanagan are the same person**, Mike's younger sibling. Older credits (Absentia, Hill House, Doctor Sleep) say James; later writing credits (Midnight Club, Usher, Carrie) say Jamie. Jamie's own interview byline gives pronouns "he/they/she"; this file uses "they." Check TMDB for a split person record. ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/1910482-jamie-flanagan), [New 32 Productions interview](https://www.new32productions.com/jamie-flanagan/), [Wikipedia: Mike Flanagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Flanagan_(filmmaker)))
- **Kyra Sedgwick is not a Flanagan actor.** She *directed* Carrie episode 5, "Physical Education." ([Wikipedia: Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series)))
- **Tom Hiddleston is in the Flanaverse.** He plays the adult Chuck Krantz in The Life of Chuck. The rest of the cast in the brief checks out: Mark Hamill (Albie Krantz), Karen Gillan (Felicia Gordon), Chiwetel Ejiofor (Marty Anderson), Jacob Tremblay (17-year-old Chuck), Mia Sara (Sarah Krantz). ([Wikipedia: Life of Chuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Life_of_Chuck))
- **Carrie (2026) cast, verified:** Summer H. Howell (Carrie), Samantha Sloyan (Margaret White), Siena Agudong (Sue Snell), Alison Thornton (Chris Hargensen), Josie Totah (Tina Blake), Arthur Conti (Billy Nolan), Joel Oulette (Tommy Ross), Amber Midthunder (Rita Desjardin), Matthew Lillard (Henry Grayle). **Rahul Kohli is in it**, recurring as Seth Peist. It premiered on Prime Video on 2026-10-07 and was renewed in October 2026. ([Wikipedia: Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series)))
- **The Exorcist: Martyrs** (Universal, set for 2027-03-12) stars Scarlett Johansson, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Diane Lane, **Jacobi Jupe** (not Jacob Tremblay), Sasha Calle, Scoot McNairy, Gil Bellows, Laurence Fishburne and John Leguizamo, plus regulars Hamish Linklater, Carl Lumbly, John Gallagher Jr., Benjamin Pajak, Carla Gugino, Rahul Kohli, Robert Longstreet, Matt Biedel, Samantha Sloyan and Kate Siegel (roles not public). TMDB has no characters for it yet, so the overlap engine will show the regulars without roles. ([Wikipedia: Exorcist: Martyrs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exorcist:_Martyrs))
- **Kate Siegel is not in The Midnight Club** per its Wikipedia cast list, and **Henry Thomas is not in The Life of Chuck** ([CinemaBlend](https://cinemablend.com/interviews/henry-thomas-not-in-the-life-of-chuck-had-plenty-say-mike-flanagan-stephen-king-adaptation)).
- **Several names in the brief have exactly one Flanagan credit** and are not "regulars" in a meaningful sense: Timothy Hutton (Hill House), Mary McDonnell and Willa Fitzgerald (Usher), Rahul Abburi (Midnight Mass), Adia and Chris Sumpter (Midnight Club). They are still worth profiling for the multi-role stories in section 3.

## 1. Profiles of the regulars

Ordered roughly by depth of involvement.

### Kate Siegel
Siegel first met Flanagan at an audition for one of his films that never got made; after her read he asked for one more take, "just for you," and she says "my acting changed forever on that day." He later called her for a small part in *Oculus* as Marisol Chavez, the mirror's victim. ([Collider](https://collider.com/midnight-mass-mike-flanagan-kate-siegel-interview/), [Yahoo](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/horror-mavens-muse-kate-siegel-154010852.html)) They married in 2016 and she co-wrote *Hush* with him, playing Maddie Young, the deaf author of a novel called *Midnight Mass*. Her roles since: Jenny Browning (*Ouija: Origin of Evil*), Sally Burlingame (*Gerald's Game*), adult Theo Crain (*Hill House*), Viola Willoughby / the Lady in the Lake (*Bly Manor*), Erin Greene (*Midnight Mass*), Camille L'Espanaye (*Usher*), Miss Richards (*Life of Chuck*), and Adelaide in *Carrie*, a mostly voice-only part. ([Wikipedia: Kate Siegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Siegel), [Wikipedia: Hush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hush_(2016_feature_film))) She has moved behind the camera too: her directing debut was "Stowaway," a Flanagan-written segment of *V/H/S/Beyond* (2024), and she directed *Carrie* episode 3. ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/kate-siegel-vhs-beyond-spoilers-shudder-2000507623), [Wikipedia: Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series))) On the troupe: "There will always be the Flanagan fam. We like to work together." ([Yahoo](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/horror-mavens-muse-kate-siegel-154010852.html))

### Samantha Sloyan
Sloyan's first Flanagan role was Sarah Greene, the friend who dies early in *Hush* (2016). Since then: Leigh Crain (*Hill House*), Bev Keane (*Midnight Mass*), Shasta (*Midnight Club*), Tamerlane Usher (*Usher*), Miss Rohrbacher (*Life of Chuck*), Margaret White (*Carrie*), and a part in *The Exorcist: Martyrs*. ([Wikipedia: Samantha Sloyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Sloyan)) Flanagan wrote Margaret White specifically for her, and at Beyond Fest 2026 he called her *Carrie* performance "the best work I've ever, ever seen from [her]." ([Wikipedia: Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series)), [TVLine](https://www.tvline.com/2278942/margaret-white-carrie-actress-samantha-sloyan/)) She worried Margaret would repeat Bev Keane; Flanagan's answer was that Margaret's love for Carrie would separate them. ([Creepy Catalog via search](https://creepycatalog.com/?p=64977), secondhand)

### Henry Thomas
The *E.T.* star's run began as Father Tom in *Ouija: Origin of Evil*, then Jessie's father Tom (*Gerald's Game*), young Hugh Crain (*Hill House*), the Overlook bartender who wears Jack Torrance's face (*Doctor Sleep*), Henry Wingrave (*Bly Manor*), Ed Flynn (*Midnight Mass*), Freedom Jack inside Natsuki's story (*Midnight Club*), and Frederick Usher (*Usher*). ([Wikipedia: Doctor Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Sleep_(2019_film)), [Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series)), [Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries))) In 2022 he credited his horror second act "entirely" to Flanagan: "he's hired me consistently since 2014... We just finished our eighth project together," adding that it has "a repertory feel." ([SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/910307/henry-thomas-on-crawlspace-e-t-at-40-his-collaborations-with-mike-flanagan-more-interview)) At Rose City Comic Con 2025 he described the cast's private game when a new script lands: fancasting each other. "Oh, he's gonna hire Trucco for that one." "Oh, this is definitely Samantha Sloyan's role." ([Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/tv-movies-mike-flanagan-haunting-of-hill-house-midnight-mass-doctor-sleep-cast-henry-thomas-rose-city-comic-con-2025))

### Carla Gugino
First collaboration: Jessie Burlingame, handcuffed to the bed for nearly all of *Gerald's Game* (2017). ([AwardsDaily](https://www.awardsdaily.com/2024/06/03/the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-star-carla-gugino-thrives-on-flanagan-collaboration-roles-that-scare-her-as-an-actor)) Then Olivia Crain (*Hill House*), older Jamie / the Storyteller (*Bly Manor*), the voice of the Judge (*Midnight Mass*), Verna (*Usher*), the news and commercial voice (*Life of Chuck*), and *The Exorcist: Martyrs*. ([Wikipedia: Bly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Bly_Manor), [Wikipedia: Midnight Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Mass_(miniseries)), [Wikipedia: Life of Chuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Life_of_Chuck)) Why she returns: "He keeps bringing things my way that pushed me, that terrify me," and "I feel really taken care of in the edit." ([AwardsDaily](https://www.awardsdaily.com/2024/06/03/the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-star-carla-gugino-thrives-on-flanagan-collaboration-roles-that-scare-her-as-an-actor)) Consequence named her its 2023 TV Performer of the Year for Verna. ([Consequence](https://consequence.net/2023/12/carla-gugino-tv-performer-of-the-year/2))

### Rahul Kohli
Flanagan announced him on Twitter in 2019 as "the remarkably charming RAHUL KOHLI" when casting Owen Sharma, the cook in *Bly Manor*. ([TV Guide](https://www.tvguide.com/news/izombie-rahul-kohli-the-haunting-of-bly-manor-netflix)) During the *Bly* shoot Flanagan pitched him *Midnight Mass* in person, not on paper: "Then he said, 'I want you to play the sheriff.'" Kohli asked that Sheriff Hassan carry Malcolm X's surname, and Flanagan had the props made. ([Inverse](https://inverse.com/entertainment/rahul-kohli-midnight-mass-sheriff)) Since then: Vincent inside Amesh's story (*Midnight Club*), Napoleon "Leo" Usher (*Usher*), Bri (*Life of Chuck*), Seth Peist (*Carrie*), and *The Exorcist: Martyrs*. Flanagan has called him "one of my favorite actors (and favorite people)." ([Fangoria](https://www.fangoria.com/the-familiar-faces-of-fright-in-mike-flanagans-horror-universe/))

### Zach Gilford
The *Friday Night Lights* actor joined with the lead in *Midnight Mass* (Riley Flynn), announced alongside Siegel and Linklater in February 2020. ([Wikipedia: Midnight Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Mass_(miniseries))) He then played Mark, the nurse at Brightcliffe (*Midnight Club*), and 1979's Roderick Usher, the younger self of Bruce Greenwood's character (*Usher*). ([Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries))) Family overlap: his then-wife Kiele Sanchez was a staff writer on *Usher* and co-wrote the finale, "The Raven"; Gilford filed for divorce in April 2025. ([Wikipedia: Zach Gilford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Gilford), [Wikipedia: Usher episode table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries))) No direct quote found on why he returns.

### Annabeth Gish
Her first Flanagan film was *Before I Wake* (Natalie), followed by Mrs. Clara Dudley (*Hill House*), Dr. Sarah Gunning (*Midnight Mass*), and Roderick's mother Eliza Usher (*Usher*). ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/49425-annabeth-gish)) During *Midnight Mass* press she said: "This is actually my third project with Mike, because I did a film called 'Before I Wake' with him way back in the day," and called him "a really magnificent writer." ([ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/news/midnight-mass-annabeth-gish-kristin-lehman-mike-flanagan))

### Kristin Lehman
A newcomer on *Midnight Mass*, where she played Annie Flynn, Riley's mother. Before signing on, "I looked at who his repertory company is that he created," and afterward: "you just know when you've found your tribe." ([ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/news/midnight-mass-annabeth-gish-kristin-lehman-mike-flanagan)) She is a one-scene parent in *Carrie* ("Regretful Parent," per TMDB). ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/148112-kristin-lehman))

### T'Nia Miller
Two roles: Hannah Grose, Bly's housekeeper (*Bly Manor*), and Victorine LaFourcade, the surgeon daughter (*Usher*). ([Wikipedia: Bly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Bly_Manor), [Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries))) Bloody Disgusting covered her *Bly* casting as the first of the "new faces." ([Bloody Disgusting](https://bloody-disgusting.com/tv/3581446/actress-tnia-miller-new-faces-join-mike-flanagans-haunting-bly-manor/)) No quote found on returning.

### Bruce Greenwood
Gerald Burlingame in *Gerald's Game*, Dr. John Dalton in *Doctor Sleep*, an uncredited ghost in *Hill House* episode 5 (haunting his *Gerald's Game* co-star Gugino), and Roderick Usher after Frank Langella's firing (section 3). ([Wikipedia: Gerald's Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerald%27s_Game_(film)), [Wikipedia: Doctor Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Sleep_(2019_film)), [PopCulture.com](https://popculture.com/2018/10/27/the-haunting-of-hill-house-surprising-easter-egg-fans-missed-bruce-greenwood/), [AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/bruce-greenwood-replace-frank-langella-usher-flanagan-n-1848861359)) He credited "both Mikes, Mike Fimognari and Mike Flanagan" with keeping track of where Roderick was emotionally in each scene during the scramble. ([Primetimer, via search snippet](https://www.primetimer.com/interviews/fall-of-the-house-of-usher-bruce-greenwood-netflix-mike-flanagan-remorse); the page itself would not load, so treat the wording as **UNVERIFIED**)

### Carl Lumbly
Took over Scatman Crothers' Dick Hallorann in *Doctor Sleep*, then played C. Auguste Dupin (*Usher*), Sam Yarborough (*Life of Chuck*), and appears in *The Exorcist: Martyrs*. ([Wikipedia: Doctor Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Sleep_(2019_film)), [Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)), [Wikipedia: Life of Chuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Life_of_Chuck)) Flanagan cast him to evoke the original rather than imitate it; he refused both impersonation and digital de-aging for the Shining roles. ([Wikipedia: Doctor Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Sleep_(2019_film)))

### Hamish Linklater
Father Paul Hill, who is also the de-aged Monsignor Pruitt, in *Midnight Mass*. He auditioned with the first three scripts plus nine pages of monologues and cited Raúl Juliá in *Romero* as his model. ([Wikipedia: Midnight Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Mass_(miniseries))) He returns in *The Exorcist: Martyrs*. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exorcist:_Martyrs))

### Michael Trucco
John Stanley, the neighbor killed early in *Hush*; Wade Scarborough (*Midnight Mass*); Frederick inside Amesh's story (*Midnight Club*); Rufus Griswold (*Usher*); Dylan's dad (*Life of Chuck*, per Screen Rant); John Hargensen (*Carrie*). ([Wikipedia: Hush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hush_(2016_feature_film)), [Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series)), [Wikipedia: Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series)), [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/carrie-tv-show-cast-mike-flanagan-returning-actors/)) He is the subject of Henry Thomas's fancasting line, "Oh, he's gonna hire Trucco for that one." ([Popverse](https://www.thepopverse.com/tv-movies-mike-flanagan-haunting-of-hill-house-midnight-mass-doctor-sleep-cast-henry-thomas-rose-city-comic-con-2025))

### Robert Longstreet
Barry the Chunk of the True Knot (*Doctor Sleep*), Horace Dudley (*Hill House*), Joe Collie, the island drunk (*Midnight Mass*), the janitor (*Midnight Club*), William Longfellow (*Usher*), and *The Exorcist: Martyrs*. ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/79416-robert-longstreet), [Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series))) TMDB lacks the Midnight Club credit; Wikipedia has it. No quote found.

### Matt Biedel
Sturge (*Midnight Mass*), Tim (*Midnight Club*), William "Bill-T" Wilson (*Usher*), Doctor Winston (*Life of Chuck*), a voice role in *Carrie*, and *The Exorcist: Martyrs*. ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/588335-matt-biedel)) No quote found.

### Katie Parker (also credited Catherine Parker)
The longest-serving actor of all after Siegel: Callie, the lead's sister, in *Absentia* (2011), the $70,000 Kickstarter feature that started it all. ([Wikipedia: Absentia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absentia_(film))) Then a phone-store clerk (*Oculus*), Poppy Hill (*Hill House*), Silent Sarey (*Doctor Sleep*), Perdita Willoughby, sister of Siegel's Viola (*Bly Manor*), Aceso (*Midnight Club*), and Annabel Lee (*Usher*). ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/551462-katie-parker), [Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series))) She has played the sister of a Flanagan lead twice, fourteen years apart.

### Lulu Wilson
Doris Zander, the possessed little sister in *Ouija: Origin of Evil* (2016); young Shirley Crain (*Hill House*); and teenage Madeline Usher in the opening of *Usher*. ([Wikipedia: Ouija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouija:_Origin_of_Evil), [Wikipedia: Hill House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Hill_House_(TV_series)), [Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries))) She thinks Flanagan had her in mind for Shirley because they already knew each other. ([Loeschtwins interview, via search](https://loeschtwins.com/the-haunting-of-hill-house-star-lulu-wilson-on-mike-flanagan-why-she-likes-horror-and-those-insane-long-takes)) Of her *Usher* day: "I'm only in the first half of the first episode," covered in water, blood and dirt, "a distressed goose." ([SlashFilm](https://slashfilm.com/1299897/lulu-wilson-distressed-goose-mike-flanagans-fall-of-the-house-of-usher)) Before release, SlashFilm called the role undisclosed. The teenage-Madeline credit comes from Wikipedia's cast list.

### Mark Hamill
Arthur Gordon Pym, the Ushers' fixer (*Usher*), then Albie Krantz, Chuck's grandfather (*Life of Chuck*). ([Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)), [Wikipedia: Life of Chuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Life_of_Chuck)) He came in as a self-described "Flanafan" of *Hill House*, *Bly* and *Midnight Mass*, and his wife Marilou, who doesn't watch horror, talked him into it after he read the scripts: "You're not even going to watch this!" ([SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/1424950/the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-mark-hamill-mike-flanagan-fan), [Netflix Tudum](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/mark-hamill-the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-interview)) SlashFilm reports he told THR Flanagan was the reason he came out of semi-retirement from live action. ([SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/1981038/star-wars-mark-hamill-retired-netflix-the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher), secondhand)

### Sauriyan Sapkota
Amesh (*Midnight Club*), then Prospero "Perry" Usher, whose masked party opens the killings (*Usher*). ([Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series)), [Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries))) One of three *Midnight Club* teens promoted into *Usher* (with Ruth Codd and Igby Rigney, plus Aya Furukawa in a smaller part).

### Ruth Codd
A barber and makeup artist with no acting experience, Codd was found on TikTok, where she posted about makeup and disability, by *The Midnight Club*'s casting team. Like her character Anya, she is a below-the-knee amputee. ([Looper](https://www.looper.com/1417427/the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-who-plays-juno-ruth-codd-how-discovered)) Flanagan on her episode: "Ruth crushes that episode." ([TVLine](https://tvline.com/2022/10/08/midnight-club-episode-7-recap-mike-flanagan-interview-anya-dies/)) She returned as Juno, Roderick's young second wife (*Usher*), and is in the recurring cast of *Carrie*. ([Wikipedia: Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series)))

### Igby Rigney
Warren Flynn, Riley's altar-boy brother (*Midnight Mass*); Kevin, Ilonka's love interest (*Midnight Club*); Toby (*Usher*, recurring). ([Wikipedia: Midnight Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Mass_(miniseries)), [Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series)), [Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)))

### Alex Essoe
Recast Shelley Duvall's Wendy Torrance in *Doctor Sleep*; then Charlotte Wingrave (*Bly Manor*), Mildred Gunning (*Midnight Mass*), Poppy Corn inside Natsuki's story (*Midnight Club*), and a court witness (*Usher*). ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/210454-alex-essoe)) Her Mildred went through five makeup phases as the character de-ages; Trevor Macy: "She has five phases, everybody else has three, for obvious reasons." ([TheWrap](https://thewrap.com/midnight-mass-mildred-de-aging-makeup-digital-effects-mike-flanagan))

### Elizabeth Reaser
Alice Zander, the widowed medium in *Ouija: Origin of Evil*, then adult Shirley Crain (*Hill House*), whose younger self was Lulu Wilson, her *Ouija* daughter. ([Wikipedia: Ouija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouija:_Origin_of_Evil), [Wikipedia: Hill House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Hill_House_(TV_series))) On *Ouija*'s practical effects: "It makes it harder sometimes because you're working with magnets and candles and Ouija boards." ([Fangoria](https://www.fangoria.com/the-familiar-faces-of-fright-in-mike-flanagans-horror-universe/))

### Oliver Jackson-Cohen and Victoria Pedretti
The two *Hill House* siblings who came back in new roles for *Bly Manor*: Jackson-Cohen as Luke Crain, then Peter Quint; Pedretti as Nell Crain, then the au pair Dani Clayton. ([Wikipedia: Bly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Bly_Manor)) Neither has worked with Flanagan since *Bly*, and Flanagan has said "there are no plans for more chapters" of *The Haunting*. ([Fangoria](https://www.fangoria.com/the-familiar-faces-of-fright-in-mike-flanagans-horror-universe/))

### Crystal Balint
Dolly Scarborough, Wade's wife (*Midnight Mass*); Maggie (*Midnight Club*); Morella Usher, Frederick's wife (*Usher*); recurring in *Carrie*. ([Wikipedia: Midnight Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Mass_(miniseries)), [Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series)), [Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)), [Wikipedia: Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series))) Michael Trucco was her on-screen husband in *Midnight Mass*.

### Courtney Bell
Lead of *Absentia* (Tricia Riley, opposite Katie Parker as her sister), an auctioneer in *Oculus*, and Andrea Morgan in *Before I Wake*. ([TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/person/572407-courtney-bell), [Wikipedia: Before I Wake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Before_I_Wake_(2016_film))) She and Flanagan were in a relationship before he married Siegel and have a son together. ([Wikipedia: Mike Flanagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Flanagan_(filmmaker))) No credits with him since 2016.

### Timothy Hutton
Older Hugh Crain in *Hill House*, the present-day half of a role shared with Henry Thomas. His only Flanagan credit. ([Wikipedia: Hill House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Hill_House_(TV_series)))

### Mary McDonnell and Willa Fitzgerald
Madeline Usher in 2023 (McDonnell) and 1979 (Fitzgerald) in *Usher*; one Flanagan credit each. Lulu Wilson and Kate Whiddington play her as a teen and a child. ([Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)))

### Doug Jones
The creature actor appears in the very first Flanagan feature, as Walter Lambert in *Absentia* (2011), and returned under prosthetics as Ghoul Marcus in *Ouija: Origin of Evil*. ([Wikipedia: Absentia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absentia_(film)), [Wikipedia: Ouija](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouija:_Origin_of_Evil))

### Heather Langenkamp
The *Nightmare on Elm Street* star played Dr. Georgina Stanton, who runs Brightcliffe hospice (*Midnight Club*), then Vera Stanley (*Life of Chuck*). Flanagan announced her casting himself on Twitter. ([Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series)), [Wikipedia: Life of Chuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Life_of_Chuck))

### The Midnight Club teens: Aya Furukawa, Adia, Chris Sumpter, Annarah Cymone, Rahul Abburi
- **Aya Furukawa:** Natsuki (*Midnight Club*), then Tina (*Usher*, recurring). ([Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)))
- **Adia:** Cheri (*Midnight Club*). Only Flanagan credit found.

## 3. One actor, many roles: casting stories inside a single work

### The Haunting of Hill House (2018): every Crain is two people
Each Crain child has a child actor and an adult actor (Paxton Singleton/Michiel Huisman, Lulu Wilson/Elizabeth Reaser, Julian Hilliard/Oliver Jackson-Cohen, Mckenna Grace/Kate Siegel, Violet McGraw/Victoria Pedretti). Hugh Crain is split across Henry Thomas (1992) and Timothy Hutton (present). ([Wikipedia: Hill House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Hill_House_(TV_series))) The series' central twist is that the Bent-Neck Lady who haunts young Nell is adult Nell, so Pedretti effectively plays her own ghost. ([Wikipedia: Hill House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Hill_House_(TV_series))) Hidden-ghost Easter egg: Bruce Greenwood, Gugino's husband from *Gerald's Game*, appears uncredited as a ghost in Olivia and Hugh's room in episode 5, confirmed by Flanagan to Variety. ([PopCulture.com](https://popculture.com/2018/10/27/the-haunting-of-hill-house-surprising-easter-egg-fans-missed-bruce-greenwood/))

### The Haunting of Bly Manor (2020): the same company, new people
Pedretti, Jackson-Cohen, Henry Thomas, Siegel, Katie Parker and Gugino all return in unrelated roles; the two series share no story. ([Wikipedia: Bly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Bly_Manor))
- **Carla Gugino** is the unnamed Storyteller at a 2007 wedding rehearsal for most of the series; the finale reveals she is older Jamie, the gardener played by Amelia Eve. ([Wikipedia: Bly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Haunting_of_Bly_Manor))
- **Kate Siegel** plays Viola Willoughby, who becomes the faceless Lady in the Lake. Most of the faceless Lady was performed by stunt performer Daniela Dib, partly because Siegel had a young baby at home. ([TVLine](https://tvline.com/2020/10/12/the-haunting-of-bly-manor-kate-siegel-interview-viola-ghost/))
- **Henry Thomas and Alex Essoe** play Henry and Charlotte Wingrave. **Katie Parker** plays Perdita, Viola's sister.

### Doctor Sleep (2019): recasting The Shining without imitating it
Flanagan rejected both digital de-aging and impersonation for the Overlook flashbacks. Carl Lumbly took Hallorann, Alex Essoe took Wendy, and Roger Dale Floyd played young Danny. ([Wikipedia: Doctor Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Sleep_(2019_film)))
- **Henry Thomas** fills Jack Nicholson's space: credited as the bartender (Lloyd) who wears Jack's face, he also plays Jack in flashbacks, and the director's cut adds the hotel caretaker. Nicholson was reportedly invited to cameo and declined. ([Wikipedia: Doctor Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Sleep_(2019_film)))
- **Danny Lloyd**, the original Danny, came back for a single day after about 38 years away from acting, as a spectator at the baseball game. Flanagan reached him on Twitter. ([Wikipedia: Doctor Sleep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Sleep_(2019_film)))

### Midnight Mass (2021): makeup over pixels
- **Hamish Linklater** is billed as "Father Paul Hill / Monsignor Pruitt (dual role)." The young priest is the elderly monsignor, restored to youth. ([Wikipedia: Midnight Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Mass_(miniseries)))
- **Alex Essoe's** Mildred Gunning goes through five makeup phases as she de-ages, done by Fractured FX rather than digital. Flanagan: "we both still feel like that technology is distracting." ([TheWrap](https://thewrap.com/midnight-mass-mildred-de-aging-makeup-digital-effects-mike-flanagan))
- **Quinton Boisclair** plays the Angel under wings and full creature makeup and trained with movement coach Terry Notary. ([Fangoria](https://www.fangoria.com/monsterpiece-theater-part-five-a-conversation-with-quinton-boisclair-the-angel-midnight-mass/))
- **Carla Gugino** is present only as the Judge's voice. ([Wikipedia: Midnight Mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Mass_(miniseries)))

### The Midnight Club (2022): stories within the story
The hospice teens tell nightly stories, and the cast doubles as the characters in them. Guest regulars appear only inside those tales: Henry Thomas as Freedom Jack and Alex Essoe as Poppy Corn (Natsuki's story), and Rahul Kohli as Vincent and Michael Trucco as Frederick (Amesh's story). Wikipedia does not map every club member's story roles, so the full grid is **UNVERIFIED**; the TMDB episode credits should fill it in. ([Wikipedia: Midnight Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Midnight_Club_(TV_series)))

### The Fall of the House of Usher (2023)
- **Carla Gugino as Verna.** The character is never named on screen. "Verna" is an anagram of "Raven," and she takes eight forms, one per episode and Usher child: a bartender, a masked party guest, a hunched security guard, and in one scene an ape. Gugino: "It was important for me to never have this be some sort of master-of-disguises costume show. Rather, these are the ways in which these characters can receive Verna." ([AwardsDaily](https://www.awardsdaily.com/2024/06/03/the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-star-carla-gugino-thrives-on-flanagan-collaboration-roles-that-scare-her-as-an-actor), [Uproxx](https://uproxx.com/tv/who-is-verna-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-carla-gugino), [Daily Beast](https://www.thedailybeast.com/wildest-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-scene-carla-gugino-plays-an-ape/))
- **Four-age characters.** Roderick: Lincoln Russo (child), Graham Verchere (teen), Zach Gilford (1979), Bruce Greenwood (2023). Madeline: Kate Whiddington, Lulu Wilson, Willa Fitzgerald, Mary McDonnell. Dupin: Malcolm Goodwin (1979), Carl Lumbly (2023). ([Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)))
- **The Langella firing.** Frank Langella was cast as Roderick on 2021-12-09. Filming began 2022-01-31. In mid-April 2022, with the shoot about halfway done, Netflix fired him after an investigation found "unacceptable conduct on set." Langella disputes this and says the accusations were never proven. Production kept filming around Roderick's scenes. Flanagan announced "my dear friend, the brilliant Bruce Greenwood" by the end of the month (Deadline, 2022-04-29). All of Langella's footage was scrapped and reshot over roughly the next two and a half months, and the shoot wrapped 2022-07-09. ([Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)), [TV Insider](https://tvinsider.com/1043579/frank-langella-the-fall-of-the-house-of-usher-netflix-fired-misconduct-reaction), [TVLine](https://www.tvline.com/casting-news/fall-of-house-of-usher-bruce-greenwood-recast-roderick-frank-langella-fired-1234824974/), [AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/bruce-greenwood-replace-frank-langella-usher-flanagan-n-1848861359)) Kate Siegel later said of Greenwood's arrival: "palpable relief from everybody... We redid almost all of it. It's amazing what he did." ([Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/fall-house-usher-star-recasting-183100530.html))
- **Behind the camera:** Flanagan directed episodes 1, 2, 5 and 6; Michael Fimognari directed 3, 4, 7 and 8 and shot all eight. ([Wikipedia: Usher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_House_of_Usher_(miniseries)))

### The Life of Chuck (2024)
Chuck is four people: Cody Flanagan (age 7), Benjamin Pajak (11), Jacob Tremblay (17), Tom Hiddleston (adult). Pajak returns in *The Exorcist: Martyrs*. ([Wikipedia: Life of Chuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Life_of_Chuck), [Wikipedia: Exorcist: Martyrs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Exorcist:_Martyrs))

### Carrie (2026)
Summer H. Howell plays Carrie, with Juliet Rose Foley as the younger version. Samantha Sloyan plays Margaret, with Theo Flanagan as the younger version. More than 1,400 actresses auditioned; Flanagan called it the "most exhaustive and difficult casting experience" of his career. Howell first spoke to Flanagan on April Fools' Day and thought it was a prank. ([Wikipedia: Carrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_(TV_series)), [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2278726/mike-flanagan-carrie-casting-most-difficult-experience-career/))
